How to play

  1. Enter your name

    Type it in Your name on the Play tab. Your finished games then count towards your record and rating on the Stats tab. Leave it empty to play without saving.

  2. Pick an opponent

    Choose one of the seven levels and a color, then press New game. Start low and move up when you begin winning.

  3. Make your moves

    Drag a piece, or tap it and then tap where it should go. You can also type moves like e4, Nf3 or O-O. New to chess? Turn on Coach mode and it rings any piece that can be taken for free.

  4. Read the review

    When the game ends, the review shows your accuracy and your biggest mistakes, each with the move you should have played. Tap one to see it on the board.

Seven opponents

Each level's rating was measured by playing it against Stockfish at fixed strengths, so treat the numbers as rough guides.

    What's here

    Play

    Games against the bot at any of the seven levels. Turn on coach mode to see which pieces can be taken for free, or ask for a hint. The page names the opening as you play.

    Puzzles

    Hundreds of tactics from real games, picked to match your puzzle rating, which goes up as you solve them.

    Learn

    Eleven short lessons for newer players, from what the pieces are worth to forks, pins, skewers and basic checkmates, each with a puzzle.

    Stats

    Everyone who plays with a name, ranked by estimated rating. Each player gets a rating chart, badges to collect, and replays of their games.

    How it works

    Paul's Chess Bot is a small chess engine written in Python. It searches ahead with alpha-beta search, remembers positions it has already scored, and plays out every capture sequence before judging a position. On the public site your browser runs that same Python code with Pyodide, so the only thing sent anywhere is the result of a game you play under your name.

    The code is open source under the GPL-3.0 license. The repository also has a terminal version, and a mode that plugs into chess apps like Cute Chess or Arena.
